The Jibarito sandwich

A serving suggestion inspired by the sandwich in our bakery’s menu.

  1. Prepare fresh bread, ham, sliced tomato, lettuce and cheese.
  2. Slice the bread and lightly toast it until golden.
  3. Layer the cheese, ham and vegetables on the bread.
  4. Close the sandwich and serve while the bread is warm.
